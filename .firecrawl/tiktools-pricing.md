## What's Included In Every Paid Tier

Signatures, request signing, priority routing and premium stream paths are bundled into every paid tier - no add-ons, no surprise charges. Each plan's published price is the all-in price.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I switch plans?

Yes - upgrade, downgrade, or change billing cycles anytime from the Pricing page. Changes take effect immediately on your next billing cycle.

### What happens if I exceed my daily limit?

Requests are stopped at your daily cap. No overage charges, no surprise invoices. If you need more headroom, upgrade to the next tier from the Pricing page - changes take effect immediately.

### Do you offer refunds?

No - paid plans are non-refundable. The free Sandbox tier exists so you can verify the product before paying. Partial refunds are only issued when a service is provably broken on our side (sustained outage, billing error) for the affected period. We do not refund based on testing, scraping, or change of mind - data + proxy + bandwidth costs are consumed the moment a key is active.

### How do API keys work?

After subscribing, your API key is automatically generated and available in your Dashboard. Each key has its own subscription and usage limits. You can purchase additional keys, each with its own plan.

### Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel from the Billing page and your subscription continues until the end of the current period. No penalty, no fees.

### How do I manage my subscription?

Everything is managed through your Billing page - view subscriptions, update payment methods, download invoices, and cancel plans. No external portals needed.

### What is the Sandbox tier?

Sandbox is the free tier - 5,000 requests/day, 20 requests/min, 50 WS sessions per 24h, up to 3 concurrent streams, 2 hours max per session, no credit card. Server signs only - your IP connects directly to TikTok (no shared proxy pool). Endpoints that require server-side scraping (resolve\_user\_ids, agency-grade Gifter Leaderboard) are paid-only. Community is the legacy alias for the same tier.

### How do captions work?

Captions are sold as standalone subscription plans by hours of stream time, not credit packs. Casual ($7/wk for 12h or $29/mo for 60h), Pro ($15/wk for 30h or $59/mo for 140h), Extreme ($29/wk for 60h or $99/mo for 260h). 1 hour = 60 minutes of real-time transcription or translation. Auto-renews, cancel any time. No API key required.
